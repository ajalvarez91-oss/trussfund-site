Straight Answers

Ask the hard ones.

Is this investment advice?
No. We report what our own accounts did and show the machine's work. Every decision in your account is yours — you read the dry-run, you fire the trade. Nothing here is a recommendation.
Where does my money live?
In your brokerage account, under your login. We never touch, hold, or custody funds. There is nothing to wire and nobody to trust with a balance.
Which numbers are real money?
The test account is paper until around October 1 — labeled PAPER everywhere it appears. The $1,000 benchmark is real money, labeled REAL. When the test account goes live, the label changes and the record continues, unbroken.
Why no shorting on the Robinhood track?
Robinhood doesn't support it. Rather than pretend, the Robinhood track runs the long-only book the platform is actually good at — index spine plus the income pyramid. The full long/short stack is the Alpaca track.
What can go wrong?
You can lose money. Markets go down, strategies underperform, drawdowns happen — that's why the worst drawdown is printed in red above instead of buried in a footnote. If you need certainty, this isn't the place; nobody honest sells certainty.
What's the AI here?
A disciplined pipeline plus an AI council that argues in the open. The dry-run table is their work, shown before anything happens. No characters, no black box, no "the algorithm says trust me."
